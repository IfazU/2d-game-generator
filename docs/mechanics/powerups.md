# Powerups

**Does:** tracks temporary speed, invulnerability, jump, or damage multipliers. **Use:** short-lived pickups. **Avoid:** permanent skill trees or stacked RPG effects. **Configure:** kind, multiplier, duration. **Depends:** none. **Modify:** application in `src/game/**`; protect manager. **Example:** `powers.activate('speed', 1.5, 5000)`.
