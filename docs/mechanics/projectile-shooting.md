# Projectile Shooting

**Does:** pools and fires projectiles with speed, cooldown, damage data, and lifetime. **Use:** bullets, lasers, fireballs. **Avoid:** beam or physics-rope weapons. **Configure:** texture, speed, cooldown, damage, lifetime. **Depends:** damage when hits should reduce health. **Modify:** collision effects under `src/game/**`; protect the pool. **Example:** `shooting.fire(x, y, angle)`.
